## 2. Service Description

Cunny is an **interactive AI education mobile application** designed for K-12 students (ages 11–14 / SMP) in Indonesia. The App provides:

- **Structured AI courses** covering topics such as Introduction to AI, Machine Learning basics, and related subjects
- **Interactive lessons** with quizzes, visual content, and gamified learning experiences
- **Fruit Scanner** — an on-device AI feature that demonstrates machine learning concepts using your device camera
- **Gamification system** — XP, levels, streaks, energy, badges, and leaderboards to motivate consistent learning
- **Progress tracking** — cloud-synced learning progress across sessions

The App is available on Android via Google Play Store.
